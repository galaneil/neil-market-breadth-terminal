Movers

Every name that moved at least your threshold over the period you pick, from the terminal's own daily prices. Change the numbers and the list updates. Click a sector chip or use the picker to narrow to the groups you care about. Very small, thinly traded names are filtered out by default so the list stays tradable.

Download for TradingView

Move to TradingView: in its watchlist menu choose Import list and pick the downloaded file, or paste the copied symbols into a list. TradingView has no public way for another program to write to a watchlist, so this is a one-click hand-off, not a live link. To bring a TradingView list back, use Export list there and paste it below.
